# **App Name**: HealPulse

## Core Features:

- Mandatory Medical Disclaimer: An introductory safety modal ensuring users acknowledge the information is for educational purposes only and not professional medical advice.
- AI Symptom Tool: A feature that utilizes a logic-based tool to interpret natural language symptom descriptions in both Hindi and English to suggest matching homeopathic remedies.
- Bilingual Materia Medica: A comprehensive database of homeopathic medicines with bilingual support (Hindi/English) for usage, potency, and dosage instructions.
- Cloud-Synced Saved List: Securely save and retrieve favorite remedies across sessions using a database for long-term storage.
- Category-Based Navigation: Fast-filtering UI allowing users to jump to specific remedies for fever, skin problems, or digestion via visual buttons.
- Dosage Alarms: A local notification utility to set timed reminders for recurring medication schedules.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Botanical Forest Green (#2D864B), selected to evoke a sense of natural health and tranquility.
- Background: Pale Mint Frost (#F5F9F6), providing a clean, light, and clinical medical environment that remains easy on the eyes.
- Accent color: Meadow Lime (#62994D), used for primary call-to-action buttons to maintain an analogous harmony while adding energy.
- Main font pairing: 'PT Sans' for all headlines and body text to provide a humanist, approachable, and highly readable look in both English and Hindi scripts.
- A bottom-anchored tab bar navigation for ergonomic one-handed use, paired with a modern card-based content layout.
- Soft fade-in transitions for modal disclaimer overlays and smooth vertical list sorting animations when filters are applied.
- Simple, monolinear stroke icons with rounded ends to communicate a gentle and supportive brand identity.
